Producer-ready MIDI from public domain sources

MIDI chord ideas you can drop straight into the piano roll.

A tool for producers who want royalty-free MIDI chord and melody blocks derived from public domain musical works, reduced into simple playable piano-roll patterns. Listen, copy the notes, download MIDI, and adapt the loop in Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, GarageBand, or any DAW built around a piano roll.

Think in blocks

Royalty-free MIDI chord and melody blocks, reduced from public domain musical works and translated into piano-roll patterns producers can use.

Step 1: Pick a vibe Choose a mood such as dark minimal, ambient pad, emotional chord progression, lo-fi keys, melodic techno, cinematic tension, sad piano loop, or warm chord stabs.
Step 2: Listen to the loop Press Listen to hear the MIDI chord progression as a simple synth preview. It is a quick way to hear the harmonic shape before you use your own instruments.
Step 3: Read the blocks Stacked notes are chords. Notes moving left to right are the sequence. Longer blocks hold for longer. The grid shows the same producer-ready MIDI data used for playback.
Step 4: Download or copy Download the MIDI file or copy the note names into your DAW piano roll. Use it as a MIDI chord pack, melody starter, chord progression idea, ambient pad chords, lo-fi keys, melodic techno chords, chord stabs, or a dark minimal loop with your own sounds.
